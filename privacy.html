Privacy policy

legion-rclone is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, to copy the owner's own files to the owner's own Google Drive account through the Google Drive API.

  • It accesses no Google account other than the owner's.
  • It does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any user data with anyone.
  • Files it copies go only to the owner's Google Drive and stay under the owner's control there.
  • The owner can withdraw its access at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

Questions can be raised by opening an issue in the repository that hosts this page.
